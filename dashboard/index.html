    How this radar values a launch

    implied value = TVL × category multiple
    × size × momentum × maturity × breadth

    Category multiple
    Median market cap / TVL of the category's priced protocols, shrunk toward the median of all (). Under 3 priced: that global median.
    Size
    Market cap grows as TVL across the comparables; half of that discount is applied to larger protocols.
    Momentum
    Half of the 7-day TVL change, clamped to ±%.
    Maturity
    Days listed while recording no token; full at 3 years.
    Breadth
    +3% for each doubling of the chain count.

    Every input is a field of the snapshot; no protocol is named in the model. A value is a claim, not a forecast of a launch.
